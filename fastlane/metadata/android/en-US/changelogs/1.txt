RetroFrame 0.2.0 - a rewrite for speed and reliability.

Faster: the folder is read in one query instead of thousands of calls, and watched by the system instead of polled every 10 seconds. Photos decode to fit your screen.

Fixed: a crash on Android 5.1. Shuffle now works, and off sorts naturally. Favouriting no longer jumps the slideshow.

New: fade, slide and zoom transitions. A proper icon.

Not yet tested on a physical tablet - reports very welcome.
